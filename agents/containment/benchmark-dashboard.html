What these results establish

This measures containment planning after trusted observations arrive. It does not establish raw-traffic detection accuracy, real firewall efficacy, MITRE endorsement, or council superiority. Cases are seeded variants of authored response families, not independent real-world attacks. Each result records its own suite and completion count. Attack rates, service dependencies, a 600-second horizon and a 120-second deadline are benchmark assumptions. Network isolation does not stop local ransomware encryption. Counts and failures remain visible for incomplete runs. Compare model runs only with matching dataset and simulator hashes; machine load can affect latency.
